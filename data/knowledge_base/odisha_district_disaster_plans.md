# Odisha District Cyclone Vulnerability & Resource Planning Directory

## Coastal High-Vulnerability District Profiles

### Puri District
- **Coastal Distance**: 0 km (Direct coastal frontline)
- **Population (Census 2011)**: 1,698,737
- **Literacy Rate**: 84.67%
- **Vulnerability Level**: EXTREME
- **Historical Analog**: Fani (2019), Phailin (2013), 1999 Super Cyclone
- **Key Infrastructure**: Puri Port, Grand Road shelters, Chilika lake fishing villages
- **Priority Protocol**: Immediate evacuation of coastal fishing hamlets within 5 km of coast. Deploy ODRAF unit 1 & 2 at Puri town and Brahmagiri.

### Jagatsinghpur District
- **Coastal Distance**: 0 km (Direct coastal frontline)
- **Population (Census 2011)**: 1,136,971
- **Literacy Rate**: 86.59%
- **Vulnerability Level**: EXTREME
- **Historical Analog**: 1999 Super Cyclone (Ersama epicenter), Fani (2019)
- **Key Infrastructure**: Paradip Port, Indian Oil Refinery, Ersama shelter network
- **Priority Protocol**: Secure industrial chemical storage at Paradip Port. Evacuate Ersama, Balikuda, and Kujang blocks to elevated MPCS shelters.

### Kendrapara District
- **Coastal Distance**: 0 km (Direct coastal frontline)
- **Population (Census 2011)**: 1,440,289
- **Literacy Rate**: 79.17%
- **Vulnerability Level**: EXTREME
- **Historical Analog**: Fani (2019), Yaas (2021), 1999 Super Cyclone
- **Key Infrastructure**: Bhitarkanika Mangrove ecosystem, Rajnagar shelters, Dhamra approach
- **Priority Protocol**: Mangrove protection zone acts as natural surge buffer, but Rajnagar, Mahakalapada blocks require total evacuation due to tidal inlets.

### Bhadrak District
- **Coastal Distance**: 0 km (Direct coastal frontline)
- **Population (Census 2011)**: 1,506,337
- **Literacy Rate**: 82.78%
- **Vulnerability Level**: HIGH to EXTREME
- **Historical Analog**: Yaas (2021 landfall area), Amphan (2020), 1999 Super Cyclone
- **Key Infrastructure**: Dhamra Port, Chandbali coastal block
- **Priority Protocol**: Deploy high-capacity water pumps at Chandbali and Dhamra. Coordinate with NDRF for boat rescues in flooded river basins.

### Balasore District
- **Coastal Distance**: 0 km (Direct coastal frontline)
- **Population (Census 2011)**: 2,320,529
- **Literacy Rate**: 79.79%
- **Vulnerability Level**: EXTREME
- **Historical Analog**: Yaas (2021), Amphan (2020), 1999 Super Cyclone
- **Key Infrastructure**: Chandipur Missile Testing Range, Subarnarekha river mouth, Sadar coastal belt
- **Priority Protocol**: Evacuate Remuna, Sadar, and Bhograi coastal blocks. Prepare river embankment repair units along Subarnarekha river.

### Ganjam District
- **Coastal Distance**: 0 km (Direct coastal frontline)
- **Population (Census 2011)**: 3,529,031
- **Literacy Rate**: 71.09%
- **Vulnerability Level**: EXTREME
- **Historical Analog**: Phailin (2013 Gopalpur landfall), Hudhud (2014)
- **Key Infrastructure**: Gopalpur Port, Berhampur urban cluster, Rushikulya river mouth
- **Priority Protocol**: Mobilize medical response teams in Berhampur. Evacuate Chhatrapur, Chikiti, and Ganjam coastal blocks.

## Inland Neighboring District Profiles (Secondary Impact Zone)

### Khordha District (Bhubaneswar Capital Region)
- **Coastal Distance**: ~20-40 km
- **Population (Census 2011)**: 2,251,673
- **Vulnerability Level**: HIGH
- **Primary Hazards**: High wind speed (120-160 km/h causing tree falls, urban power grid shutdown), roof damage, waterlogging.
- **Protocol**: Keep Bhubaneswar airport & AIIMS emergency road corridors clear using tree-cutter response teams.

### Cuttack District
- **Coastal Distance**: ~30-50 km
- **Population (Census 2011)**: 2,624,470
- **Vulnerability Level**: HIGH
- **Primary Hazards**: Mahanadi river flooding, severe urban waterlogging in Millennium City Cuttack, agricultural crop submergence.
- **Protocol**: Monitor Mahanadi river barrage levels at Jobra. Pre-position dewatering pumps across city lowlands.

### Mayurbhanj District
- **Coastal Distance**: ~60-100 km (North interior hill region)
- **Population (Census 2011)**: 2,519,738
- **Vulnerability Level**: MODERATE
- **Primary Hazards**: Torrential rain causing flash floods in Budhabalanga river, landslides in Similipal national park region.
- **Protocol**: Flash flood alert for Baripada town and tribal hilly hamlets.
