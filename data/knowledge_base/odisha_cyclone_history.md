# Odisha Historical Cyclone Knowledge Base

## Cyclone Fani (May 2019)
- **Year**: 2019
- **Category**: Extremely Severe Cyclonic Storm (ESCS)
- **Max Wind Speed**: 215 km/h (115 knots / 133 mph)
- **Landfall Location**: Puri, Odisha (3 May 2019)
- **Central Pressure**: 932 hPa
- **Affected Districts**: Puri, Khordha, Cuttack, Jagatsinghpur, Kendrapara, Ganjam, Bhadrak, Balasore
- **Impact Summary**: Caused 89 deaths in India, severe damage to coastal infrastructure, electricity grid outage across 14 districts, destroyed over 500,000 houses in Puri and Khordha.
- **Disaster Response**: Early evacuation of over 1.2 million people within 24 hours to 4,000 shelter centers, recognized globally by UN-ISDR for effective disaster management.

## Cyclone Phailin (October 2013)
- **Year**: 2013
- **Category**: Very Severe Cyclonic Storm (VSCS)
- **Max Wind Speed**: 200 km/h (110 knots)
- **Landfall Location**: Gopalpur, Ganjam District, Odisha (12 October 2013)
- **Central Pressure**: 940 hPa
- **Affected Districts**: Ganjam, Puri, Khordha, Gajapati, Nayagarh, Kandhamal, Cuttack
- **Impact Summary**: Affected over 13 million people, severe agricultural crop loss across 500,000 hectares, flooded low-lying coastal regions.
- **Disaster Response**: Massive evacuation of 1.15 million people reduced casualties to 45, setting a national benchmark for zero-casualty target policies.

## Odisha Super Cyclone (October 1999)
- **Year**: 1999
- **Category**: Super Cyclonic Storm (Category 5 equivalent)
- **Max Wind Speed**: 260 km/h (140 knots)
- **Landfall Location**: Ersama, Jagatsinghpur District, Odisha (29 October 1999)
- **Central Pressure**: 912 hPa
- **Affected Districts**: Jagatsinghpur, Kendrapara, Cuttack, Puri, Bhadrak, Balasore, Jajpur, Khordha
- **Impact Summary**: 9,885 fatalities officially recorded, devastating storm surge up to 5-7 meters penetrating 20 km inland, total destruction of communication lines and coastal ecology.
- **Legacy**: Catalyzed the establishment of OSDMA (Odisha State Disaster Management Authority) and modern early warning systems.

## Cyclone Hudhud (October 2014)
- **Year**: 2014
- **Category**: Very Severe Cyclonic Storm (VSCS)
- **Max Wind Speed**: 185 km/h (100 knots)
- **Landfall Location**: Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh (impacted southern Odisha districts)
- **Central Pressure**: 960 hPa
- **Affected Districts**: Koraput, Malkangiri, Rayagada, Gajapati, Ganjam
- **Impact Summary**: Heavy rainfall up to 250 mm caused flash floods and landslides in southern Odisha high-altitude tribal districts.

## Cyclone Amphan (May 2020)
- **Year**: 2020
- **Category**: Super Cyclonic Storm
- **Max Wind Speed**: 240 km/h (130 knots)
- **Landfall Location**: Sundarbans (West Bengal), passed close to northern Odisha coast
- **Central Pressure**: 920 hPa
- **Affected Districts**: Balasore, Bhadrak, Kendrapara, Jagatsinghpur, Mayurbhanj
- **Impact Summary**: Heavy gale winds (100-120 km/h) and coastal surge damaged power lines, trees, and saline inundation in agricultural fields across northern Odisha.

## Cyclone Yaas (May 2021)
- **Year**: 2021
- **Category**: Very Severe Cyclonic Storm (VSCS)
- **Max Wind Speed**: 140 km/h (75 knots)
- **Landfall Location**: South of Balasore (Dhamra port area, Bhadrak/Balasore border, Odisha)
- **Central Pressure**: 970 hPa
- **Affected Districts**: Balasore, Bhadrak, Kendrapara, Mayurbhanj, Keonjhar
- **Impact Summary**: High tidal waves, widespread inundation in coastal villages, heavy rainfall leading to swollen rivers in northern Odisha.

## Cyclone Gulab (September 2021)
- **Year**: 2021
- **Category**: Cyclonic Storm
- **Max Wind Speed**: 95 km/h (50 knots)
- **Landfall Location**: Kalingapatnam (Andhra Pradesh / Odisha border)
- **Central Pressure**: 992 hPa
- **Affected Districts**: Ganjam, Gajapati, Rayagada, Koraput, Malkangiri
- **Impact Summary**: Moderate wind damage, widespread heavy rainfall causing localized flooding in southern interior districts.
