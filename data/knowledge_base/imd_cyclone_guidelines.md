# IMD Cyclone Classification & Disaster Management Guidelines

## IMD Cyclone Intensity Categorization Scale
- **Low Pressure Area (LPA)**: Wind speed < 17 knots (< 31 km/h)
- **Depression (D)**: Wind speed 17–27 knots (31–49 km/h)
- **Deep Depression (DD)**: Wind speed 28–33 knots (50–61 km/h)
- **Cyclonic Storm (CS)**: Wind speed 34–47 knots (62–88 km/h)
- **Severe Cyclonic Storm (SCS)**: Wind speed 48–63 knots (89–117 km/h)
- **Very Severe Cyclonic Storm (VSCS)**: Wind speed 64–89 knots (118–165 km/h)
- **Extremely Severe Cyclonic Storm (ESCS)**: Wind speed 90–119 knots (166–220 km/h)
- **Super Cyclonic Storm (SuCS)**: Wind speed ≥ 120 knots (≥ 221 km/h)

## IMD 4-Stage Cyclone Warning Protocol
1. **Pre-Cyclone Watch (72 Hours Prior)**: Issued by IMD when a depression forms in Bay of Bengal with potential to affect Odisha coast. Early advisory to ports, fishermen, and state disaster response forces.
2. **Cyclone Alert (48 Hours Prior)**: Issued 48 hours before expected landfall. Specifies location of storm, intensity, and coastal districts likely to be affected. Pre-positioning of ODRAF and NDRF teams starts.
3. **Cyclone Warning (24 Hours Prior)**: Issued 24 hours prior to landfall. Gives precise landfall location estimate, wind speeds, storm surge height, and district-level impact details. Mandatory evacuation orders enacted in vulnerable 0-5 km coastal belts.
4. **Post-Landfall Outlook (12 Hours Prior & During Landfall)**: Tracks inland propagation, weakening rate, heavy rainfall zones, potential river flooding, and landslides in interior hill tracts.

## Storm Surge & Coastal Risk Mapping
- **Extremely High Risk Zone**: Coastal belt of Jagatsinghpur, Kendrapara, Bhadrak, Balasore, Puri (Storm surge height > 5 meters possible for SuCS/ESCS).
- **High Risk Zone**: Ganjam, Khordha coastal tracts (Storm surge 2.5 - 5 meters).
- **Moderate Inland Risk**: Cuttack, Jajpur, Mayurbhanj, Nayagarh (High wind damage 80-120 km/h, intense rainfall > 200mm, flash flooding).

## Odisha Disaster Mitigation & Protocol Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)
- **Multi-Purpose Cyclone Shelters (MPCS)**: 800+ reinforced cyclone shelters along Odisha's 480 km coastline designed to withstand wind loads up to 300 km/h and storm surge flooding.
- **Evacuation Standard**: mandatory evacuation of pregnant women, elderly, children, and livestock to MPCS shelters 24 hours before landfall.
- **Inter-Agency Coordination**: State Crisis Management Group led by Special Relief Commissioner (SRC) Odisha, with real-time sync between IMD, OSDMA, Air Force, Coast Guard, and District Collectors.
- **Communication & Power Resilience**: Pre-deployment of satellite phones, HAM radios, and mobile DG sets at all block offices to ensure uninterrupted communication during grid collapse.
